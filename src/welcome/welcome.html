4Step 4 of 5

Optional: a live background

Put a moving background behind the chat. Pick one:

  • Weather sky follows the real weather and time of day where you are. Choose your place first.
  • Fluid is dark coloured smoke that swirls, glows and follows your cursor. It needs no place.

Choose it in Settings › Weather › Background. You can switch it off there, or from the bar's panel.
